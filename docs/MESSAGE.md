Suggested drafts for you to approve, change, or replace. Each one uses only lines you have already approved.

- **Do you replace our software?**
  - Draft: "No. We work alongside the systems you already use. BASIS is the software our data team uses to do the work."
  - Please confirm: does a department need its own BASIS login or contract? If yes, the answer must say so.
- **Which systems do you work with?**
  - The answer in the table is your approved draft from the brief. Please approve it.
- **Do you make decisions about athletes?**
  - Draft: "No. Readiness, load, and return-to-play decisions stay with your coaches and clinicians. We collect, check, connect, and explain the data they use."
- **How do you protect athlete data?**
  - Partial draft: "You decide who can see athlete data. You set the rules and we follow them."
  - Then add: where files are stored, who on your side can open them, and what happens to the data if a department leaves.
  - Do not name FERPA, HIPAA, SOC 2, GDPR, or any other standard unless you can show proof.
- **What does it cost?**
  - Only you can answer this.
  - If there is no fixed price yet, one honest option is: "We set the price after the first call, based on your sports and systems."
